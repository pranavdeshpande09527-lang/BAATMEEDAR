Baatmeedar is built around this simple principle: seek the truth, speak the truth, and reject misinformation.

Baatmeedar is an intelligent platform designed to help users understand and verify information available online. In a world where misinformation can spread quickly, Baatmeedar makes it easier to analyze claims, articles, and videos and get a clearer picture of what is trustworthy.

Users can provide information in different ways—by entering a statement directly, sharing an article link, or providing a YouTube video link. Baatmeedar processes the provided content, analyzes the claims, and presents the findings in a simple and understandable way.

Our goal is to make fact-checking accessible, transparent, and easy for everyone. Baatmeedar helps users think critically, identify misleading information, and make better-informed decisions in the digital world.
